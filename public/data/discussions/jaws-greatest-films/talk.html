The other claims you cite are not unsourced, and they are different in nature. It is just a point of fact that Jaws is the prototypical summer blockbuster, and that aspect is covered in substantial detail throughout the article, and attributed to many sources. How "great" something is is a subjective opinion, not a fact. Firstly, a subjective opinion should not be offered in Wikipedia's voice, it should be attributed. Secondly, just because we can source a few lists of great films in which Jaws appears does not inherently make it one of the greatest films of all-time, in just the same way a bunch of positive reviews doesn't necessarily make a film well reviewed. We can say the claim has been made by this person or that list (which is what the article does) but we should refrain from drawing our own conclusions. Thirdly, proclaiming something the "greatest" (or one of) is an exceptional claim, which should require WP:EXCEPTIONAL sources, which simply do not exist in the article for this specific claim.
