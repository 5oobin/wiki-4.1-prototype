Olympic Winter Games, not Winter Olympic Games

[edit source]

The correct, official name of these games is the "Olympic Winter Games", not the "Winter Olympic Games", which has been the case since 1926. Aside from it being the correct official name, there is an important distinction in the Winter Games versus the Summer Games (more commonly known as the 'Olympic Games'). IMO the article should be retitled accordingly, likewise all internal references throughout the article should be amended. Chris W. (talk) 03:35, 8 February 2026 (UTC)

We go by what reliable sources say. I have just looked at several media reports, and ALL of them say "Winter Olympic Games". HiLo48 (talk) 05:07, 8 February 2026 (UTC)
What reliable sources are you referring to? I would contend those are incorrect usages, and may be conflating things like "Winter Olympics" and "Winter Olympic Games". The former is fine, the latter isn't.
Use of "Winter Olympic Games" notwithstanding, Wikipedia itself takes great care to always include the official name in articles for each respective OWG (e.g. from the 2030 article: The 2030 Winter Olympics (French: Jeux olympiques d'hiver de 2030), officially known as the XXVI Olympic Winter Games (French: XXVIes Jeux Olympiques d'hiver)).
Google refers to it as the Olympic Winter Games. The organising body does not refer to it as the 'Winter Olympic Games', only the 'Olympic Winter Games'. Officially it is either the "Winter Olympics" or the "Olympic Winter Games" (the IOC's trademarked both).
My opinion is that Wikipedia should reflect the correct, official name as the article title, with redirects for "Winter Olympics" and "Winter Olympic Games". The article can certainly reference unofficial but popular derivations, but should be clear that the official names are either "Winter Olympics" or the "Olympic Winter Games". The official names are Olympic Winter Games™, Paralympic Winter Games™, Winter Olympics™ and so on (as evidenced by reliable sources like the OWG26 site - https://www.olympics.com/en/milano-cortina-2026/territories).
